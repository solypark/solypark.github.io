I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Economic Sciences and the Paul G. Allen School for Global Health at Washington State University.

I am a development economist working on nutrition and health in low- and middle-income countries. I investigate what causes undernutrition or poor health, what works to improve these conditions, and the role of behavior, psychology, and gender dynamics as both drivers and consequences. To answer these questions, I use field and lab-in-the-field experiments. I have ongoing or previous research in Nigeria, Niger, Madagascar, Ethiopia, India, and the Philippines.

Before joining WSU, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. I received my PhD in Applied Economics from Cornell University.

My name is pronounced “Soul-lee.”
